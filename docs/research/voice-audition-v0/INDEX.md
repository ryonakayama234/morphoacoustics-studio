# VA-L0 — Voice Atlas / creator listening notebook

**2026-10-23. Status: IMPLEMENTED CANDIDATE / NOT SCIENTIFICALLY ADOPTED.**
Search terms: voice-atlas, auditory discrimination, speaker identity, voice-likeness, buzzy, A/A control, CC-A2, embodied physical voice.

## Why this exists

Core Experiment 025 established closed-set /a i u/ cues with conspicuously non-voice-like timbre (29/29 answered trials correct; 30th missing). The research bottleneck is not only whether two bodies produce distinct numerical pressure; it is whether a creator can hear a meaningful difference **and** recognize a voice. CC-B1 preliminary informal audition similarly warned against equating waveform difference with audibility.

We add a Studio-side observational capability using the already adopted CC-A2 two-body physical Takes. No Core physics or audio is edited. SMC and ASMR remain independent.

## Scope and gate

- Narrow supported source: two verified CC-A2 physical Takes (M0@1 versus M1@1) generated by the same frozen 3-task/LF 100Hz path.
- Creator can conduct order-masked A/B and exact-same-WAV A/A control, rate voice-likeness and artifacts, leave a note, export source-bound JSON.
- No new creator voice, no pitch/EQ/postprocessing, no naturalness claim, no arbitrary script, no generic graph editor, and no MotorPersona or new physical source.
- **Gate at this increment:** CI build/tests + real CC-A2 manual audition + receipt/provenance verification. Until manual listening and independent evaluation, label results as exploratory; do not merge based on optimistic acoustic claims.
